It contains the cascade ratio $\beta_{\mathrm{SL}}$, singularity exponent $\alpha$, Hausdorff dimension $D$, and the scaling exponents $\zeta_2$ and $\zeta_3$, and ESS exponents $\beta_2$ from the spatial bootstrap, along with their errors as the 16th and 84th percentiles of the bootstrap distribution. The row labelled \textit{Median} gives the median across the galaxy population of each of the exponents, and the corresponding errors report the distance from that median to the 16th and 84th percentiles. The $\mathcal{M}_s$ classification column gives each galaxy's classification against the six Pan and Scannapieco (2011) Mach number curves. A single Mach number is provided where one curve fits well, else the range it falls between is reported. Lastly, the table includes the range in which the scaling exponents are fitted for each galaxy.}
